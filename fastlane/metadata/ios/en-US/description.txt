America Bitcoin Wallet is a self-custody Bitcoin wallet for creating or importing wallets, sending BTC, receiving BTC, and managing your balance from your device.

Private keys are generated and stored on your device. The app does not require an account to get started.

Core features:

- Create a new Bitcoin wallet or import an existing wallet
- Send and receive BTC
- Scan QR codes for Bitcoin addresses and payment requests
- View balances and transaction history
- Use watch-only wallets to monitor funds
- Use on-device encryption and backup tools
- Check market and price information with included widgets

America Bitcoin Wallet is built for straightforward Bitcoin self-custody and everyday send / receive use.
